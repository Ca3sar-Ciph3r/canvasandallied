WE DO REPAIRS

Over time, even the toughest covers take a beating. At Canvas & Allied, we provide expert repair services for canvas and PVC tarpaulins — restoring strength, structure, and functionality to your essential gear. Whether it's a torn seam, damaged eyelet, or faded section, our team brings precision craftsmanship and industrial-grade materials to every repair. Extending the life of what protects you is what we do best.

What we repair

Repairs

Welcome to the hands-on world of canvas restoration, where every stitch brings new life to worn materials. From reinforcing seams to replacing entire panels, we revive tarpaulins, covers, and industrial textiles with care and expertise. This is craftsmanship that protects your investment — one thread at a time.

Materials

Great protection starts with the right materials. We use only premium, field-tested fabrics and components — from industrial-strength canvas to UV-stabilized PVC and specialized adhesives. Every layer is selected for its durability, flexibility, and performance in South African conditions. Because lasting protection begins at the fiber level.

Custom Projects

Where precision meets purpose. Whether it's a one-off event cover, a branded feature wall, or a fully custom canvas solution, we work hand-in-hand with clients to design, cut, and build to spec. No two needs are the same, and neither are our solutions. If you can imagine it, we can cover it.
