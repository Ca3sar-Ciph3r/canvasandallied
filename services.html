Services

SERVICES

At Canvas & Allied, we design and manufacture high-performance canvas and tarpaulin solutions across industries — from marine and caravan protection to transport, household, sports, and custom branding wraps. Our products are built to withstand South African conditions and tailored for durability, fit, and function — no matter the application.

Our service categories

BOATING

Protect your boat with custom marine covers built to handle South Africa's harsh conditions.

LEISURE

Protect your caravan year-round with heavy-duty, stormproof covers made to fit, breathe, and endure any condition.

HOUSEHOLD

Protect your home essentials with durable, stylish covers built for tough South African weather.

TRANSPORT

Trusted by pros nationwide, our transport tarps protect cargo from weather, UV, and road wear — on the move or in storage.

SPORTS

Durable and custom-fit, our sports covers protect gear and structures indoors or out — built for schools, clubs, and serious play.

INDUSTRIAL

Transform your fleet into mobile billboards with custom truck tarpaulin wraps built for impact, durability, and all-road conditions.
