Welcome to Canvas & Allied

Since 2002, Canvas & Allied has outfitted adventurers, professionals, and industry leaders across South Africa with premium canvas and tarpaulin solutions — built for durability and trusted to perform. From rugged gear for explorers and overlanders to expert products and repairs for leisure, industrial, marine, and agricultural use, we deliver strength, style, and longevity wherever the journey leads!

Our Services

Transport
We offer a full range of transport-related solutions, including taut liners, tonneau covers, side curtains, flat tarpaulins, shaped tarpaulins, load nets, load straps, tow straps, and comprehensive repair work. These products are designed to meet the demands of the transport industry, offering protection and durability on the road.
Boating
Canvas & Allied provides boat-related products such as boat covers, bikini covers, engine covers, sun canopies, general canopies, dock fenders, and fishing bags, all made with weather-resistant materials to protect your gear and vessels.
Leisure
For camping and caravanning, we create awnings, tents, teepees, marquees, ground sheets, windbreakers, waterproof covers, mattress covers, shade covers, and bags for tents, poles, and pegs. We also supply tent poles, guy ropes, and pegs, ensuring you have everything you need for a comfortable outdoor experience.
Household
We offer household solutions like gazebo covers, chair and umbrella covers, window awnings, protective covers, pond liners, carport shade cloth, carport curtains, garden refuse bags, screens, swimming pool covers, and jacuzzi covers, all designed to protect and extend the life of your home's outdoor elements.
Sports
For sports enthusiasts, we provide cricket practice nets, cricket pitch covers, soccer nets, hockey nets, water polo nets, rugby post protectors, and repair services for trampolines and jumping castles, making sure your sporting equipment stays in top condition.
Industrial
Our industrial products include welding curtains, strip curtains, ventilation ducts, bag filters, filter bags, flexible collars, chicken house curtains, trolley covers, waste bags, and laundry bags, providing protection and efficiency in various industrial environments.

Get in touch
  • What products do you manufacture?

    We specialize in manufacturing custom-made canvas and PVC products, including tarpaulins, truck covers, tents, marine and boat covers, industrial protective covers, shade ports, awnings, and outdoor furniture covers. Each item is made with precision to meet specific customer requirements.

  • Do you offer custom sizes and designs?

    Yes, all our products are custom-designed to fit your exact needs. Whether you're looking for a unique size, a particular color, or specific features, we'll work closely with you to ensure the final product is both functional and durable.

  • What materials do you use?

    We use high-quality materials such as ripstop canvas and PVC-coated fabrics, along with UV-resistant thread and heavy-duty zips. Our goal is to provide long-lasting protection and performance across various environments — from farms to factories to outdoor adventures.

  • Do you offer repair services?

    Yes, in addition to manufacturing, we offer repair services for canvas and PVC products. Whether it's a torn tarp, a broken zip, or stitching that needs reinforcement, we'll assess and repair it to restore its function.

  • Can you come to my site to measure?

    For large or complex items such as truck covers, trailer covers, or fitted awnings, we offer on-site measurement services in and around Port Elizabeth. Our team will come to you to ensure everything is measured accurately for a perfect fit.

  • What is your turnaround time for orders?

    Typical turnaround time for orders ranges between 7 to 14 working days, depending on the size and complexity of the job and current workload. We always provide clear timelines and keep you updated throughout the process.

  • How can I place an order or request a quote?

    To place an order or request a quote, you can contact us by phone (041 453 3231), email (sales@canvasandallied.co.za), or through our website's contact form. You're also welcome to visit our workshop at 29 Strang Street, Sidwell, Port Elizabeth.
